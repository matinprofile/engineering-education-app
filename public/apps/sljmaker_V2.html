Single-lap joint — step-by-step

A fully client-side single-file interactive tutorial for assembling an adhesive single-lap joint (steel substrates). Use the 3D view to perform each step.

  • Step 1 — Prepare substrates
    Pick, abrade (crosshatch) and clean with acetone towel — do not pass towel twice on same spot.
  • Step 2 — Set mould & shims
    Place substrates in mould with pins and 0.2 mm spacer shims to create gap and 25 mm overlap.
  • Step 3 — Mix adhesive
    Weigh 10 g resin (A) + 4 g hardener (B), mix in centrifugal mixer 10 s.
  • Step 4 — Apply adhesive & assemble
    Apply spatula, lower top edge first then opposite, close mould.
  • Step 5 — Cure & finish
    Cure 24 hours (accelerated simulation here) until solid joint.

Step 1 — Prepare substrates

Pick two steel substrates (2 mm × 25 mm × 120 mm). Use the crosshatch sandpaper tool to abrade the surfaces, then clean with an acetone-soaked paper towel. Do not pass the towel twice over the same spot.
Resin A: 10 g
Hardener B: 4 g
Built for learning. This is a simulation and not a replacement for lab training. Handle acetone and adhesives safely (ventilation, PPE).
Controls: Rotate/Zoom: mouse. Select objects: click. Action buttons appear per step.
