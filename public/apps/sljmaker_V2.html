Tutorial Steps

  • Step 1 — Prepare substrates Abrade (crosshatch) and clean with acetone towel.
  • Step 2 — Set mould & shims Place substrates in mould with pins and 0.2 mm shims.
  • Step 3 — Mix adhesive Weigh 10 g resin (A) + 4 g hardener (B), mix 10 s.
  • Step 4 — Apply & assemble Apply spatula, lower top edge first, close mould.
  • Step 5 — Cure & finish Cure 24 hours until solid joint.
